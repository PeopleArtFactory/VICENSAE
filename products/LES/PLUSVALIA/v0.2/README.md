# LES Æ · La plusvalía de un millón · v0.2

**Public release · 14-chapter audio encyclopedia · 2026-11-21**

This folder backs up the *public preview only* of an artistic, cultural and educational LES (Living Encyclopedic Sequence). It combines an audiobook-like player, topic-focused encyclopedia, and progressive revelation. It is not a production payment gateway.

## Open the public demo

Open [LES_PLUSVALIA_AUDIO_PREVIEW_v0.2.html](./LES_PLUSVALIA_AUDIO_PREVIEW_v0.2.html) in a modern browser. The file has no runtime package dependency. Speech synthesis uses locally available browser/device voices; audio quality varies by operating system and installed voices.

## Product contract

- **14 chapters**, of which chapters 1–6 and the beginning of chapter 7 are revealed without payment; chapters 8–14 are only visible in the index.
- Public share is **44.89% of narrated words (rounded to 45%)**, not a 45% discount.
- Canonical base retail price: **€300 + applicable VAT**, without applying a discount in this edition.
- Player includes continuous reading of available material, selectable playback speed, chapter navigation and local export of released text.
- The code-coin is a nonmonetary UX simulation, not a real entitlement, charge or verifiable fiduciary act.
- **Happy Egress† by Design**: what has been legitimately released should remain exportable, subject to explicit terms of redistribution and training rights.

## Commercial access safety

**This repository is public.** The private, complete 14-chapter edition, canonical editorial sources, internal generator and package ZIP have **not** been committed. Hiding content with CSS or shipping it in JavaScript would not protect paid chapters. Real sales require server-side entitlement and restricted delivery of paid material. SHA-256 digests in [RELEASE_MANIFEST_v0.2.json](./RELEASE_MANIFEST_v0.2.json) record local source versions without embedding their contents; checksums alone are **not** a backup.
